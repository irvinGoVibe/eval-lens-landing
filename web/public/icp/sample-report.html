Project summary what the deck claims

NimbusFreight sells route-consolidation software to mid-size freight forwarders. The deck claims $41k MRR across 11 accounts, a 9-week average integration, and a bottom-up market entry through Benelux forwarders. Two founders previously shipped a comparable dispatching product; the third joined from a customer. Pricing is validated with early customers; distribution beyond founder-led sales remains a hypothesis. Churn is not disclosed anywhere in the deck.
