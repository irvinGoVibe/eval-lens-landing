EvalLens Brand Kit
==================

Correct public spelling: EvalLens
Do not use: EvalLense

The original source filenames retain the historical EvalLense spelling.
Do not copy that spelling into public-facing text.

Included:
- Primary and reversed logo masters
- Standalone unicorn symbol and favicon exports
- Open Graph social banner (1200 x 630)
- Logo system, usage, and complete brand overview boards
- Color and typography tokens

Typography:
The EvalLens website uses the native Apple/system sans stack.
SF Pro is not bundled. Download it under Apple's license:
https://developer.apple.com/fonts/

Logo wordmark:
The wordmark is custom vector artwork. Do not recreate it with typed text.
