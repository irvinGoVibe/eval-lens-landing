## Telegram (канал, EN, broadcast + фото селфи)

We applied to Y Combinator 🦄

On July 27, a few hours before the deadline, we pressed Submit on our application for the Fall 2026 batch. Five months after the first commit, five days after our public launch.

YC runs the world's largest batch review of startup applications: tens of thousands of decks per cycle, 150–250 spots, a 1–2% acceptance rate. We build software for queues like that. Now our own deck sits in one, and we know what happens to it in there.

What we told YC:
• Feb 27: first commit (back when it was AI Jury, at the Amazon Nova hackathon)
• April: pivot to EvalLens
• July 10: first live event, 5 startups scored end-to-end, ranked leaderboard
• July 22: public launch
• 1,135 commits · 84 migrations · 1,000+ calibration runs

YC answers by August 28. We'll post the result either way, including if it's a no.
