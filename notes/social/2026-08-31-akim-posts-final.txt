On weekday mornings, our newest co-founder teaches English at a school in Silicon Valley.

In the evenings he teaches at the university where he earned his doctorate. As of this week, the hours in between belong to EvalLens.

Meet Dr. Kim Golubev.

Startups usually add co-founders who ship code or close deals. We added a scholar of judgment. On purpose.

A scoring system is a theory of judgment with a UI. At EvalLens, AI prepares the evidence and a human makes the final call. Kim spent ten years on that boundary: what happens to agency and responsibility when humans are no longer the only actors in the room.

The short version of his CV:

• Ph.D. in English Literature, UNLV, 2025
• The first Crimean Tatar to earn a Ph.D. in the United States
• UNLV Outstanding Teaching Award, 2025
• "Posthuman Vital Materialism" forthcoming from Palgrave Macmillan
• Teaches English at a school in the middle of Silicon Valley

The timing is deliberate. Next week we unveil the next chapter of EvalLens. Kim joined because of what comes next.

When I asked what he could commit to, he said: "I don't want to promise what I won't deliver. But I'm ready to deliver everything I can."

That's the hire.

Welcome aboard, Kim.
