When your deck enters our competition, it isn't skimmed once and forgotten. It's read against a fixed rubric, dimension by dimension, with every score tied to something a reviewer can point at on a slide. AI prepares that analysis; a human makes the call. The founders who do best aren't the ones with the prettiest decks — they're the ones who made the deck easy to verify.

This guide walks through exactly what to put on each slide so the rubric can find it.

## The one rule that decides everything

A claim is only worth the evidence a stranger could verify without you in the room. Ambition without proof rounds down. A modest claim with a checkable number rounds up.

## What the rubric scores

- **Problem** — is the pain real, and for whom?
- **Solution** — does it obviously address that pain?
- **Market** — is the size honest and reasoned, not just a big number?
- **Traction** — what actually happened, with dates and figures?
- **Team** — why you, backed by evidence?
- **Ask & use of funds** — specific, tied to milestones?
