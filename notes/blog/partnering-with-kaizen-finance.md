Ask anyone who has taken a hundred projects to market what they run out of first. It isn't capital, and it isn't founders. It's careful reading. When your ecosystem moves that fast, every deck gets a decision — but not every deck gets an argument for the decision.

That's the gap this announcement closes. EvalLens is partnering with **Kaizen Finance**.

```text
Kaizen ecosystem decks → EvalLens methodology (6 lenses) → evidence-backed reports → your decision
```

AI prepares the evaluation. People make the final call. That line doesn't change — it's the whole product. What changes is who gets to stand behind it.

## Who we're partnering with

**Kaizen Finance** is a launch platform that has taken **100+ projects to market** — a working ecosystem of founders, incubators, and the funds that watch them. Not a directory: a pipeline that has been shipping since before "token launch" was a job title.

We live one step earlier in the same funnel. Before anyone decides anything about a startup, somebody has to actually read the deck — all of it, the same way every time. That's us. Our methodology has sharpened over **1,000+ evaluation runs**, and every report shows its evidence: which slide is carrying the company, and which one is quietly costing them the round.

## What actually changes

**For Kaizen's ecosystem:** portfolio and incubator companies get structured, evidence-backed evaluation with a human making the final call. A founder walks away knowing *why* — not just whether.

**For EvalLens users:** a bridge into a wider deal ecosystem. More decks, more runs, a sharper read for everyone — that's how the methodology got better through its first thousand runs, and it will keep getting better the same way.

**For your workflow:** nothing. Your rubric, your reports, your final call — untouched. We're deliberately boring about this: a partnership should add a capability, not renovate your process.

## A pitch competition, this autumn

The loudest part comes last. This autumn, we're co-organizing a pitch competition with the Kaizen ecosystem on the EvalLens platform — real judges, a live leaderboard, and a full evidence-backed report for every team that enters.

If you've read our [Nha Trang recap](/blog/evallense-goes-live-in-nha-trang), you know the format: the room reads the evidence together, argues, and decides. The machine prepares. The people rank. Details as the date firms up.

## The quiet part

Partnerships in this industry tend to be press releases about logos. We'd rather this one be judged the way we judge a deck: by what it makes possible, with evidence attached.

Here's the claim, stated plainly: if you run a portfolio, your companies now have a shorter path to a report that tells them exactly where their story holds and where it leaks. And if you're a founder inside the Kaizen ecosystem — your next rejection will at least come with a reason, and your next yes with a receipt.

If that's your situation, [book a demo](/demo). Bring a deck you already have an opinion about. We enjoy being checked.
