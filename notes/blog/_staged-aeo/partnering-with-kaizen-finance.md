## Common questions

**What does the EvalLens partnership with Kaizen Finance include?** Kaizen Finance is a launch platform that has taken 100+ projects to market. Under the partnership, its portfolio and incubator companies get structured, evidence-backed evaluation through EvalLens, with a human making the final call. It starts with a pitch competition this autumn on the EvalLens platform.

**What do founders in the Kaizen ecosystem get from EvalLens?** A report that explains why, not just whether. Every report shows its evidence: which slide is carrying the company and which one is quietly costing them the round. Your next rejection comes with a reason, and your next yes with a receipt.

**Does this partnership change anything for existing EvalLens users?** Your rubric, your reports, your final call stay untouched. What users gain is a bridge into a wider deal ecosystem: more decks, more runs, and a sharper read for everyone, which is how the methodology improved through its first 1,000+ evaluation runs.

**When is the Kaizen and EvalLens pitch competition?** This autumn, with real judges, a live leaderboard, and a full evidence-backed report for every team that enters. The room reads the evidence together, argues, and decides; the machine prepares, the people rank. Details will be published as the date firms up.
