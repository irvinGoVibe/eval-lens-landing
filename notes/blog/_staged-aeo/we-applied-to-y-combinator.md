## Common questions

**Did EvalLens get into Y Combinator?** We don't know yet. We submitted our application for the Fall 2026 batch on July 27, and decisions for on-time applications land by August 28. We'll publish the outcome either way, since that's the deal with building in public.

**What is EvalLens?** EvalLens is AI deal screening for accelerators and VC funds. It produces structured first reads for large piles of applications, with every conclusion backed by evidence a reviewer can check. AI prepares the analysis; a human makes the decision.

**What traction did EvalLens have when it applied to YC?** Five months of work, condensed: 1,135 commits, 84 database migrations, and 1,000+ evaluation runs spent calibrating the judging panel. A VC fund that watched our July live event signed a letter of intent to pilot EvalLens on its own deal flow in September, and Crypto Executives signed on as our first distribution partner.

**What happens if YC says no?** Nothing changes. The September fund pilot is in preparation, the next live events are being scoped, and the product keeps shipping. If it's a yes, the batch runs October through December in San Francisco, and we'll be there.
