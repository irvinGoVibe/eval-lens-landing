## Common questions

**Why is EvalLens partnering with Kaizen Finance and R2 Copilot?** Because the most common question funds ask us is "where do the decks go?" R2 Copilot gives us an isolated AI lane for organizers whose policies rule out public AI tools, and Kaizen Finance brings a working ecosystem that has taken 100+ projects to market. Same methodology, same six lenses; what changes is where the computation can happen and how many decks flow through it.

**Can I use EvalLens if my compliance team bans public AI tools?** That's the point of the R2 Copilot integration. We're wiring an option where deck analysis runs on R2's isolated infrastructure instead of public pipes, with the same six-lens methodology and the same evidence-backed report. The integration is in progress now; we announced it early because users ask about it roughly weekly.

**What is R2 Copilot?** R2 Copilot builds private AI environments; the pitch, in their words, is AI that works where public AI isn't allowed. Their published materials describe encryption on the user's side, no training on user data, and infrastructure that keeps content off the public internet. R2 is a certified Microsoft Solutions Partner.

**Is there an EvalLens pitch competition coming?** Yes, this autumn, co-organized with the Kaizen ecosystem on the EvalLens platform. Real judges, a live leaderboard, and a full evidence-backed report for every team that enters. The machine prepares, the people rank, and we'll publish details as the date firms up.
