## Common questions

**What is the Build with Gemini XPRIZE?** A $2,000,000, 90-day competition run by XPRIZE with Google and Devpost. It launched at Google I/O on May 19, 2026 and closes for submissions on August 17. It rewards a real business that operates with AI, meaning real users, real revenue, and proof that AI is doing real work inside the operation, not a demo.

**What is EvalLens entering the XPRIZE with?** A new, AI-operated line of work, not a repackage of the existing product. The rules allow a new project to run through an existing legal entity, but the business itself has to be created after the submission window opened. Ours is built on the evaluation methodology we hardened across internal pitch-deck review runs, stood up as a service people can actually use and pay for.

**How is the Build with Gemini XPRIZE judged?** Three criteria carry equal weight: Business Viability (real customers, revenue by month, a sustainable model), AI-Native Operations (AI executing real work in production), and Category Impact (moving a real category and scaling). Judges grade evidence and are not required to test the product. For us the AI-native part comes with one condition we keep everywhere: AI runs the operations, the final decision stays human.

**What are the prizes in the Build with Gemini XPRIZE?** The ladder runs $500k for first place, $200k for second, and $100k each for third through fifth, plus fifteen $50k runner-up awards and separate $50k category awards. The field is large and the 90-day window is short. We are clear-eyed about the odds, and entering anyway, because the contest grades the thing we care about: evidence.
