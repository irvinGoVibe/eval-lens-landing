## Common questions

**Can I use EvalLens if we're not allowed to use public AI tools?** That's exactly what this partnership fixes. For organizers whose compliance teams can't sign off on public AI, we're wiring an option where deck analysis runs on R2 Copilot's isolated infrastructure instead. Same methodology, same six lenses, same evidence-backed report, different pipes.

**What is R2 Copilot?** R2 Copilot builds private AI environments; the pitch, in their words, is AI that works where public AI isn't allowed. Their published materials describe encryption on the user's side, no training on user data, and infrastructure that keeps content off the public internet. R2 is a certified Microsoft Solutions Partner.

**Is the EvalLens and R2 Copilot integration live yet?** It's in progress now. We announced it early because our users ask about it roughly weekly, usually in a lower voice after the demo. We'd rather say "we're building the answer" than keep pointing at a paragraph in our privacy policy.

**Does running on isolated infrastructure change how EvalLens evaluates decks?** No. The methodology, sharpened over 1,000+ runs, stays the same, and every report still ties its conclusions to evidence. AI prepares the evaluation; you make the final decision. The only thing that moves is where the computation happens.
