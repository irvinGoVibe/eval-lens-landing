## Common questions

**What is the EvalLens and Crypto Executives partnership?** Crypto Executives, a network that describes itself as the largest crypto C-level community in the world, is adding EvalLens as the evidence layer for its deal flow. Decks moving through the CE network run through our six judging lenses and come back as evidence-backed reports, with every conclusion tied to a specific slide. The ranking and the final call stay with the humans.

**What does Crypto Executives actually do?** Four things, per their own model: hands-on advisory that takes founders from early stage to fundraise-ready over one to three months, a deal flow board connecting projects with investment entities, a KOL marketplace with 300+ influencers, and a steady calendar of demo days and ecosystem events. Their specialty is triage at speed, which is exactly why a careful evidence layer slots in without changing how anyone works.

**Does EvalLens decide which projects get funded through the CE network?** No. AI prepares the evaluation; people make the final call, and that line survives every partnership we do. At demo days, judges get a shared leaderboard and structured reports, but the ranking belongs to the humans in the room.

**What do founders in the Crypto Executives network get from this?** A report before their first advisory call that shows which claims hold and which slides are carrying the deck. The founder walks in knowing their weak points, the advisor skips the first hour of discovery, and everyone gets the same afternoon back.
