## Common questions

**How does a live EvalLens scoring session work?** In one straight line: show the product, walk the methodology out loud before scoring a single deck, hear the pitches, then open each report and work it as a room. The cohort reads the evidence together, argues the strengths and soft spots, and sometimes overrules what the system found. AI prepares the analysis, people make the call.

**What do founders get from an EvalLens evaluation?** Three things: a readiness verdict on what could already move an investor and what to close before the raise, a quality read split across six dimensions (problem, solution, market, business model, team, feasibility), and an EvalLens score from 0 to 10 that is comparable across everyone who pitched. In Nha Trang, that score was settled by the room, not the machine.

**Does the AI hand down the final score at EvalLens events?** No. The report scores each deck and shows the evidence, then the room decides, sometimes agreeing with what the system found and sometimes overruling it. The ranking every founder took home was one a room of people stood behind, not a number a model produced on its own.

**Why did EvalLens run its first live event in Nha Trang?** Because early, rough, local decks are an honest stress test, and starting somewhere forgiving teaches you nothing. Five early-stage teams pitched at A2 Hub on July 10, the two-hour session ran for almost three, and nobody left early. The day ended with a leaderboard, prizes for every team, and a venture fund signing a letter of intent to pilot EvalLens on its own deal flow.
