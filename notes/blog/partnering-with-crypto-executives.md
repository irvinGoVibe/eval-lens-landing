Ask the team at Crypto Executives what disqualifies a project from their pipeline, and the list is short enough to memorize. Everything else — any stage, any vertical, any geography — they have an answer for. Early stage? Advisory. Mid stage? Deal flow board. Ready to raise? A network of funds, syndicates, and angels that picks up the phone.

That speed is the whole point of their business. It's also the thing we found fascinating, because we live on the opposite end of the same problem. They move decks fast. We read decks carefully. A partnership was the obvious sentence to finish.

Today, it's signed. EvalLens is partnering with **Crypto Executives**.

```text
CE network → pitch decks → EvalLens 6 lenses → evidence report → human decision
```

AI prepares the evaluation. People make the final call. That line survives every partnership we do — this one included.

## Who we're partnering with

[Crypto Executives](https://cryptoexecutives.io) describes itself as **the largest crypto C-level community in the world** — a network of executives and co-founders across the web3 space, built around one promise: "through our network we solve the pain of every web3 project."

In practice that means four things: hands-on advisory that takes founders from early stage to fundraise-ready over one to three months; a [deal flow board](https://t.me/CEDealFlowChannel) connecting projects with investment entities; a KOL marketplace with **300+ influencers**; and a steady calendar of demo days and ecosystem events. Founders come in with a deck. They leave with a cap table. If you want to watch the flow itself, they run it in the open — on [Telegram](https://t.me/CEDealFlowChannel), on [X](https://x.com/Crypto_Execs), and on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/crypto-executives/).

What CE doesn't do — by design — is deep analysis. Their model is triage at speed: figure out which service a project needs and route it there. Which is exactly where a six-lens, evidence-backed evaluation slots in without changing how anyone works.

## What we're building together

Three tracks, in the order they're already happening.

**An evidence layer for the deal flow.** Decks moving through the CE network run through EvalLens — six judging lenses, every conclusion tied to a specific slide, a report in minutes instead of an afternoon. In the first days of working together, more than fifty decks arrived. The pipeline is not hypothetical.

**Demo days with a leaderboard.** CE runs events where projects pitch and investors score. We're wiring EvalLens in as the evaluation layer: every team gets a structured, evidence-backed report, judges get a shared leaderboard, and the ranking stays where it belongs — with the humans in the room.

**Founders who arrive prepared.** The quiet track, and maybe our favorite. Before a founder's first advisory call, an EvalLens report shows which claims hold and which slides are carrying the deck. The founder walks in knowing their weak points. The advisor skips the first hour of discovery. Everyone gets the same afternoon back.

Add co-marketing across both audiences, and that's the shape of it.

## The part that's already working

Partnerships usually get announced first and tested later. This one ran backwards. Before the ink dried, the CE team had opened doors across their network — including the introduction that became our [Kaizen Finance and R2 Copilot partnership](/blog/partnering-with-kaizen-finance-and-r2-copilot) last week.

A network that produces results before the agreement is signed tends to produce results after it, too. That's the bet, and for once it comes with a track record attached.

## The mission, stated plainly

Crypto moves faster than diligence. That gap is usually filled with instinct, reputation, and whoever talks loudest on a demo day. We think it should be filled with evidence — and we think the people best positioned to make that normal are the ones who already sit at the center of the deal flow.

So the joint mission is simple: every project that moves through this network shows up with a structured, evidence-backed read. Founders learn what's weak before an investor finds it. Investors spend their attention on the decks that earned it. And the final decision, every single time, belongs to a person.
