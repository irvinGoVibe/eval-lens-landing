## Короткое описание (~50 слов, для карточек)

> EvalLens is an AI-assisted evaluation layer for selection programs: pitch competitions,
> hackathons, accelerators, VC open calls, grants and corporate challenges. A panel of six
> independent AI judges reads every submission against one rubric, ties every score to
> evidence, and surfaces disagreement. The final ranking always stays with your jury.

## Длинное описание (~150 слов, для профилей)

> EvalLens gives selection programs a structured first read of every application before the
> human decision. Instead of one model issuing one opinion, six independent AI judges each
> score their own zone of a fixed rubric, blind to each other. Every finding points back to
> the page it came from, so a score can be walked back to its evidence. When judges split by
> 3.0 points or more, the disagreement is flagged rather than averaged away, marking exactly
> where human attention matters most.
>
> The scoring arithmetic is deterministic: the same inputs produce the same number on re-runs,
> which makes a shortlist defensible to founders, boards and auditors. The AI total stays
> advisory; leaderboards rank on the score a person signs. Built for pitch competitions,
> hackathons, accelerators, VC open calls, angel networks, grant programs, corporate
> innovation challenges and public tenders. Available through a limited partner program,
> with onboarding in one working session.
